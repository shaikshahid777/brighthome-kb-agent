# BrightHome Cleaning Services - Pricing & Policy

## One-Time Deep Clean Pricing
- Studio / 1 Bedroom: $89
- 2 Bedroom: $119
- 3 Bedroom: $149
- 4+ Bedroom: $179

## Recurring Service Discount
Weekly or biweekly recurring service is billed at 15% off the one-time deep clean rate for the matching home size.

## Add-On Services Pricing
- Inside oven: $25
- Inside refrigerator: $20
- Interior windows: $30

## Late Cancellation Fee
Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment incur no fee.
Cancellations made within 24 hours of the appointment incur a $35 late cancellation fee.

## No-Show Policy
A missed appointment with no cancellation notice is charged the full service price.

## Satisfaction Guarantee
If unsatisfied with a cleaning, contact BrightHome within 24 hours of the service for a complimentary re-clean of the affected area.
