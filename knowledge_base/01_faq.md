# BrightHome Cleaning Services - FAQ

## Cleaning Services Offered
BrightHome offers one-time deep clean, recurring weekly or biweekly service, and move-in/move-out cleaning.

## How to Book a Cleaning
You can book through the website booking form, by phone, or through this support line.

## Do I Need to Be Home During the Cleaning
No. Most customers provide entry instructions, such as a lockbox code or key location, in advance.

## Cleaning Supplies
Yes. BrightHome brings standard eco-friendly cleaning supplies. Customers do not need to provide any.

## What to Do Before the Cleaner Arrives
Clear clutter from surfaces and floors, and secure pets in a separate room.
