# BrightHome Cleaning Services - Service Area & Eligibility

## Serviced ZIP Codes
- 60601
- 60602
- 60603
- 60611
- 60614
- 60618
- 60622

ZIP codes not listed above are not currently serviced by BrightHome.

## Service Type Eligibility
BrightHome provides residential cleaning only. Commercial and office cleaning are not offered.

## Property Access Requirement
The property must have safe, reasonable access (working utilities, a clear path to entry) for the scheduled service to proceed.
